# ADR-0009 · Competition originality and cross-entry

**Status** [BLOCKER] Pending written organizer rulings · **Date** 18 November 2026 WIB

[Parent RFC](../rfc/RFC-0001-DJAHIT-RECONSTRUCTION.md)

## Decision

Hold each submission pending organizer confirmation of derivative use and disclosure terms. A dual submission needs both organizers to explicitly permit cross-entry, including the effect of public repo/hosting during the first event. Record each written ruling privately with full terms, date/time WIB, authorized sender, affected release and disclosure requirements. Product work can proceed independently after architecture approval.

## Alternatives

Submitting after a rewrite without a ruling risks inaccurate declarations. If permission is denied, unanswered, ambiguous or conflicts across events, hold the affected submission. Single-event entry requires its own valid clearance and consistency with all publication conditions.

## Consequences and verification

Team lead tracks prior-work and cross-entry permission independently for each event. S1 p. 9 and S2 PDF p. 6 establish prior-work restrictions. S3 PDF p. 14 documents prior Djahit competition use. Verify qualifying roster, submission format, timing and accurate originality text before release.
