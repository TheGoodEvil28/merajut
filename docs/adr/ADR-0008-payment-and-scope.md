# ADR-0008 · Payments and excluded scope

**Status** [PROPOSAL] Pending review · **Date** 18 November 2026 WIB

[Parent RFC](../rfc/RFC-0001-MARAJUT-RECONSTRUCTION.md)

## Decision

Offer cash-on-collection as an optional collection arrangement. Initial MVP records work status only and presents no settled state. If a future release records cash, use a separate provider-scoped actor/time receipt with correction audit. Digital settlement requires its own ADR and verified provider event/reconciliation. Defer YOLO, blockchain, rewards, logistics automation, offline sync and pricing AI.

## Alternatives

Carrying forward the checkout page would preserve its false success signal. A gateway expands compliance, integration and reconciliation testing.

## Consequences and verification

The demo can confirm a quote and work completion with accurate wording. Test success views and APIs for payment misrepresentation.

## Owners and open decisions

Product lead · Confirm collection practice, excluded features and how cancellation after work begins is handled.
