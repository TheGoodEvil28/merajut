# Merajut · discovery, conversation and the tailor’s workday

## Product gap at the start of the transition

On 19 November 2026, Chrome opened the earlier tailoring app on port 3100 as a guest, a customer and the tailor at Jahit Rina. The RFC, ADR register, implementation roadmap, guidebook and route implementations explain what happens after the screens below. The target column comes from the product owner's transition request. The referenced `docs/product/MERAJUT-RECONCEPT.md` was absent from this checkout at inspection. Document-specific phase boundaries and visual direction still need reconciliation.

| Surface | Observed before implementation | Target for this slice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage and shop discovery | The “Pakaian lama, rasa baru” campaign led into search, category filters and three published services. Neither the hero nor the listings explained how a conversation becomes an agreed job. | Introduce Merajut and give visitors clear entry points to discovery, the web-to-chat journey and the business workspace. Keep listings, starting prices and filters driven by published records. |
| Customer | Pesanan Saya grouped actionable and historical orders. Chat and notices sat together under “Simulasi” below the order list. The simulator’s browser title was in English. | Keep quote decisions and tracking in the account. Make the optional conversation route identifiable, explain what it can do, and identify where messages go before entry. |
| Tailor | Ringkasan showed requests, active work and collection. Assigned conversations could be found through the navigation or the secondary queues. Profile and services already controlled what a customer sees. | Put waiting conversations alongside daily work, with the actual scoped count and a direct route into the relevant queue. Connect shop management to the published storefront. |
| Shared identity | The navigation, account mail and system actor displayed the previous branding. The demo catalog mixed Indonesian descriptions with English fixture names. | Provide consistent Merajut identity, Indonesian product copy and examples, readable mobile controls and an identifiable local simulation. Preserve shop-authored text and historical records. |

The existing product opens a request from the web or from a verified simulation draft. Only the signed-in customer can accept a particular quotation version. Both roles then see the same order and its progression. These are working contracts on schema 005. Live WhatsApp messages, delivery receipts and field usability need separate evidence.
